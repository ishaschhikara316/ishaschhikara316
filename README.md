## 🔬 The Thread That Runs Through Everything

<div align="center">

```
        🏔️ altitude                    🧬 hepatocellular carcinoma
       hypoxia                              tumour hypoxia
      oxidative stress         ←──?──→      HIF1A signalling
     adaptation genes                       survival outcomes
```

*One organism adapts to low oxygen over millennia.*
*A tumour adapts to low oxygen over weeks.*
*What do they share? That's the question I keep returning to.*

## 🧪 Things I've Actually Done

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

**Ran survival analysis on 372 liver cancer patients**
Built a Python pipeline from scratch. Kaplan-Meier curves, Cox regression, hazard ratios. Found that HIF1A overexpression changes your odds — not gently.

</td>
<td width="50%">

**Found a target hiding in plain sight**
DEA on 115 HCC patient samples. CD133 (PROM1) lit up in every tumour vs normal comparison. A druggable surface target that keeps getting overlooked.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

**Optimised assays at odd hours**
DIPAS, DRDO. MDA oxidative stress assays. Blood gas analysis. VO₂ max. Dissected rat livers, hearts, lungs. The kind of wet lab work that teaches you what biology actually feels like.

</td>
<td width="50%">

**Asked if CRISPR can reach past scar tissue**
MSc thesis. The answer: the bottleneck isn't the editing — it's getting there. Fibrotic microenvironments are the real wall. That insight came from months of reading and arguing with the data.
